# Privacy Policy for TypeShift

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026

TypeShift is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture.

## 1. Data Collection

TypeShift does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or user identifiers.

## 2. Local Storage

All extension settings, selected font preferences, active site rules, and visual theme toggles are saved locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your browser.

## 3. Remote Code & Network Requests

TypeShift does not load external scripts, remote code, or third-party tracking software. All operations are self-contained within the extension.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

Since no data is collected, no data is ever shared, sold, or transferred to third parties.

## 5. Contact & Questions

If you have questions regarding this policy, feel free to open an issue on the official [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jbuilds-g/TypeShift).

> **Policy Version:** 1.0.0
